A first encounter

Play with numbers.

1

Ages 7 and up. Change a number and watch the picture—and the answer—change with it. No old notation required.

People in Edo-period Japan asked the same simple questions we do: “How many?” “How can we share them?” Questions like these are a doorway into wasan.

The plus sign means “put together.” The equals sign means both sides have the same value.

Build a triangle

One circle in the top row, two in the next, then three. How many altogether?

●

● ● ●

1 + 2 + 3 = 6

There are 6 circles altogether. Each new row has one more.

How many legs?

Change the number of cranes and turtles, then count the legs.

A crane has two legs. A turtle has four.

2 cranes● ● ● ●
1 turtle● ● ● ●

2 + 2 + 4 = 8

That makes 8 legs altogether. Each circle stands for one leg.
